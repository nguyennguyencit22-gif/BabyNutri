3. Features
Manage child profiles (create, update, view, delete).
Store child information such as name, age, gender, height, weight and allergies.
Create and manage meal plans.
Display meal plan details by day or week.
Display Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) related to child nutrition.
Connect all screens with backend APIs.
